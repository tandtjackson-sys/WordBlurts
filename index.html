About Word Blurts!

Word Blurts! is a fast-paced, free online party word game designed to test your quick-thinking skills, vocabulary, and reaction speed. Whether you are hosting a game night with friends, playing with family, or testing your own knowledge, Word Blurts! delivers instant fun right in your web browser.

How It Works

Each round generates a random category prompt and a starter letter. Once the announcer gives the signal, players race against a 10-second timer to blurt out a matching word before time runs out. Keep score right on the built-in team scoreboard and see who reigns supreme!

Key Features

  • ⚡ Instant Browser Play: No downloads, account sign-ups, or installations required.
  • ⏱️️ Dynamic 10-Second Countdown: Built-in visual and audio timer keeps the pressure on.
  • 👥 Interactive Scoreboard: Track scores for Player 1 and Player 2 or full teams seamlessly.
  • 📱 Mobile & Desktop Friendly: Fully responsive design with spacebar and touch controls.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes, Word Blurts! is 100% free to play on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

Word Blurts! works great for 1 or 2 players all the way up to large groups split into Team 1 and Team 2. The more players, the more nutty answers you'll hear, and the more hilarious it gets!

About Word Blurts!

Welcome to Word Blurts! — a digital adaptation of a tabletop word game I originally created as a teenager back in 1984, which I called Word Wit. I copyrighted the rules with the federal government and sold local bookstore and mail-order copies. Four years later, Milton Bradley released Scattergories, which went on to become an incredible hit.

The original inspiration came from my mother, who grew up playing a similar word association game in Germany. She taught us how to play, and it became a beloved family favorite over the years. The biggest drawback was always needing pencil and paper to write everything down — this digital version completely solves that issue!

Exercise for the Brain

Word Blurts! is designed for players of all ages. It offers youngsters a fun way to develop vocabulary and cognitive processing speed, while helping older adults keep their minds sharp and active — think of it as light exercise for your brain!

It is 100% free to play with no sign-ups, downloads, or accounts required.

Behind the Project & Feedback

I built this web version with assistance from Gemini over about two weeks. The text-to-speech audio rendering varies across devices — it sounds crisp on Android devices and Firefox, though iOS behavior may differ depending on system speech synthesis settings.
