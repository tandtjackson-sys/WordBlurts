How to Use Word Blurts! in Your Classroom

🔔 5-Minute Bell Ringer

Get students engaged the moment they enter. Run 2-3 fast rounds to wake up their brains and practice active recall.

🧠 Active Brain Break

Give students a quick high-energy reward between heavy-lesson blocks without losing control of the room.

🏆 Friday Team Challenge

Split the classroom into competing teams. Pick a winning number (ie. "First team to reach 20 points, wins!") Have students blurt out answers matching the letter and category before the 15-second timer runs out! Teacher keeps track of correct answers as they are blurted out, and adjusts the scoreboard accordingly after each round.

📝 Zero-Prep Sub Plans

Keep a link on your substitute teacher emergency plans. Any guest teacher can project the site and run a fun game with zero training.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Is Word Blurts! free for schools?

Yes, Word Blurts! is 100% free for teachers, students, and educational institutions.

Q: Do students need to download anything?

No. Word Blurts! is a browser-based Web Application (PWA). It works on Google Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, and all interactive display boards without downloading software.

Q: How does the game help with learning?

Category-based word association games encourage rapid lexical retrieval, expand vocabulary, reinforce initial letter sounds, and improve cognitive processing speed under light timer pressure.
